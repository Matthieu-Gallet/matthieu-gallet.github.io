Since October 2025, I am Associate Professor at the [University of Savoie Mont Blanc](https://www.univ-smb.fr/en/) in the LISTIC laboratory. I am part of the teaching team for the IDU (Informatics, Data and Usage) master's program with various courses (e.g., machine learning, deep learning, database). I conduct research in machine learning with a focus on frugal frameworks, with an application to the remote sensing domain.

Between December 2024 and September 2025, I was a Research Engineer at the [Centre de Recherches sur les Écosystèmes d'Altitude (CREA)](https://creamontblanc.org/fr/) in Chamonix, France. I was working on the development of processing chains for the analysis of vegetation in high mountain environments using Lidar and optical data. I was also involved in the development of deep learning methods for both camera traps data and acoustic sensor. I was also working on deploying and maintaining the network of automatic weather stations and camera traps in the Mont Blanc massif.

Since September 2024, I am a graduate PhD student at the [LISTIC](https://www.univ-smb.fr/listic/en/) laboratory of the [University of Savoie Mont Blanc](https://www.univ-smb.fr/en/) under the supervision of [Abdourrahmane Atto](https://www.univ-smb.fr/listic/en/presentation_listic/membres/enseignants-chercheurs/abdourrahmane-atto/), [Fatima Karbou](https://www.umr-cnrm.fr/spip.php?article380) and [Emmanuel Trouvé](https://www.univ-smb.fr/listic/en/presentation_listic/membres/enseignants-chercheurs/emmanuel-trouve/).

My research lies at the crossroads of theory and practice, developing efficient — fast, robust and frugal — algorithms to process large volumes of remote sensing data (time series or wide-footprint imagery), with applications built primarily around RADAR data (SAR and GPR).

My main contributions focus on novel algorithms — divergences between statistical distributions over image pixels, fuzzy fusion of classifiers — for detection and classification problems at large spatial scales. I apply these methods to the study of the cryosphere, in particular wet snow cover, both spatially and temporally at large scale, and to the classification of different glacier surface types.

More recently, I have focused on frugal approaches to address the energy cost and interpretability of learning algorithms, through deep learning models based on second-order statistics (covariance matrices) under geometric constraints, and through the study of self-supervised neural architectures constrained both by the non-Gaussian nature of the data and by their causal structure (temporal or volumetric series). Building on my time at CREA, I am also interested in LIDAR data for the classification of low, supra-forest vegetation.

I previously graduated from the [Institut Physique du Globe de Paris](https://www.ipgp.fr/en/) in 2021 with a Master's degree in Remote Sensing. My master's thesis was about robust inversion of Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) data and classification of GPR images.
